# Presenter Guide

The presenter is the company's marketing strategist. Notes go after the brief and are never shown to the audience.

## 1. Structure of the talk (8 to 12 minutes)

1. **Open with the bottom line (60 seconds).** Price, buyer, plan, ask. No background first.
2. **Prove the price (2 to 3 minutes).** Adjusted comps, closed vs asking, real vs nominal trend. This is where trust is won or lost.
3. **Who buys and why (2 minutes).** Segment evidence from the CRM, positioning, trust stack.
4. **How we reach them (2 minutes).** Channels, budget, funnel targets.
5. **What happens if it does not work (1 minute).** Review gates and fallback. This takes the fear out of approving.
6. **The ask (30 seconds).** Exactly what you need from this room, by when.

## 2. Converting to slides

One slide per item: (1) Bottom line, (2) Market position, (3) Pricing plan, (4) Buyers and positioning, (5) Channels and budget, (6) Funnel and gates, (7) Risks, (8) Decisions needed. Comps and execution plan go in backup slides.

## 3. Write in the notes

- A 60-second opening script in plain spoken language
- The 3 numbers to remember (for example: adjusted median, list price, budget)
- 5 to 6 anticipated questions for this audience, each with a 1 to 2 sentence answer that cites a number from the brief
- The one thing to concede if challenged, and the one thing not to concede

## 4. Question bank by audience

**Leadership**
- "Why not list at the owner's price and negotiate down?" Above +10%, inquiry drops and the listing goes stale; stale listings negotiate down further. Cite stale-listing evidence from the comps.
- "Is the budget worth it?" Show budget as % of expected fee and the cost-per-qualified-lead basis.
- "Why target diaspora?" Show CRM inquiry share and trend, not opinion.
- "What if the market turns?" Point to the Day 30 and Day 60 gates.

**Client (owner)**
- "My property is worth more than that." Walk through adjusted closed comps, then offer the agreed price test: list at X, step only if the Day 30 trigger fires.
- "Why should I pay for or approve marketing?" Show the funnel math: how many viewings it takes to produce one serious offer.
- "Can you guarantee a sale by [date]?" No. Commit to the gates, the weekly report, and the actions each gate triggers.
- "Who sees my documents?" Title type is published; documents are shared only on verified request.

**Team**
- "What do I do Monday?" Point to the execution plan owners and due dates.
- "What counts as a qualified lead?" Read the definition aloud.
- "What do I say about price?" List price and step rule only; refer negotiation to authorized negotiators.

## 5. Handling uncertainty out loud

State confidence and why in one sentence: "Confidence is medium: three of seven comps are closed sales; the rest are asking prices adjusted by our own 8% list-to-close history." Say it before someone else finds it.
