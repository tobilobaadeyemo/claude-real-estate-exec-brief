# Listing Marketing Strategy Method

Defaults below are starting points. Replace them with the company's CRM history wherever it exists and say so in the brief.

## 1. Pricing plan

| Output | Rule |
|---|---|
| List price | Hot: up to +5% over adjusted comp median (negotiation room). Warm: within +/-2% of median. Cold: at or just below median to win early traffic. Premium above +5% only with 2+ documented differentiators |
| Search-band check | Portals and buyers filter in round bands (for example ₦500M, ₦600M). If the list price sits just above a band edge, drop it below the edge |
| Floor | Owner's stated minimum. If none, adjusted comp low. If the owner floor sits above the adjusted comp high, say so plainly: the campaign is unlikely to close at that floor |
| Negotiation band | Floor to list price. Authorized negotiators only |
| Price step | One decisive step of 3 to 5%, triggered by a review gate (section 8). Avoid repeated small cuts, which signal distress |
| USD view | Show list price at the CBN official rate when diaspora is a target segment |

Lease: same logic on annual rent. Note Lagos norms on advance rent and fees, and check the Lagos Tenancy Law 2011 constraints with a solicitor.

## 2. Segments

Pick 1 primary and at most 2 secondary segments. Justify with CRM inquiry mix for the same price band, not intuition.

| Segment | Values | Objections | Where they are | Message angle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lagos senior professionals and business owners (buy to live) | Location, security, power, finish, school and office commute | Price, finish quality, service charge | Agent networks, Instagram, referrals, private viewings | Lifestyle plus peak-hour commute proof |
| Diaspora Nigerians (UK, US, Canada, UAE, Europe) | Title certainty, remote process, value in their earning currency, family use | Fraud, unverified title, delays, cannot view in person | Meta and YouTube geo-targeted, WhatsApp, diaspora networks, December home visits | Verified title, solicitor-held funds, live video viewing, USD equivalent |
| Investors (buy to let, short-let) | Yield, occupancy, resale | Yield below hurdle, service charge, short-let rules | Investor WhatsApp groups, LinkedIn, email, short-let operators | Net yield, rent evidence, management option. Verify current Lagos short-let rules before promoting short-let use |
| Corporates and expatriates (lease) | Proximity to VI and Ikoyi offices, security, power, maintenance SLA | Lease terms, upfront rent | Relocation agents, HR and admin heads, corporate leasing desks | Turnkey, maintenance SLA, flexible terms |
| Developers and land bankers (land) | Title, plot size, zoning, setbacks, access | Title defects, encroachment | Direct outreach, agent networks | Clean title pack, survey, permissible use |

## 3. Positioning

- **One line:** For [segment] who [need], [property] is the [frame] that [main benefit], because [proof].
- **Three proof points,** each checkable (spec, document, distance, data).
- **Trust stack** (essential in Lagos, decisive for diaspora): title type stated precisely; solicitor-led title search report available on request; survey plan; LASRERA-registered practitioner; physical and live-video viewings; funds held by solicitor until completion; independent valuation by a registered estate surveyor and valuer where the price is contested.
- Never publish title documents themselves. Publish the title type and offer documents on verified request (fraud risk).

## 4. Channel playbook

| Channel | Role | Best for | Primary KPI | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Portals: NPC, PropertyPro, Private Property | Capture existing demand | All sale segments | Inquiries per listing per week | One canonical listing. Co-broke agents must use the same price, photos, and description. Conflicting duplicate listings at different prices damage credibility |
| Agent co-broke network (WhatsApp agent groups) | Reach | Local buyers, investors | Agent-sourced viewings | Co-broke terms in writing before launch |
| Meta (Instagram and Facebook), Reels plus click-to-WhatsApp ads | Create demand | Local professionals, diaspora | Cost per qualified lead | Geo-target Lagos plus diaspora cities. Paid adverts need ARCON vetting |
| WhatsApp Business (catalog, broadcast to opted-in contacts) | Convert | All | Response time, lead-to-viewing rate | Response within 15 minutes in business hours. Broadcast only to contacts with consent (NDPA) |
| Google Search ads | Capture high intent | Local buyers, diaspora | Cost per qualified lead | Exact-match district plus type queries |
| YouTube walk-throughs | Create and convert | Diaspora | View-through to inquiry | 60 to 90 second cut plus 5 to 8 minute full tour |
| Email to CRM | Re-engage | Past inquirers, investors | Reply rate | Segment by budget band |
| Open house and live virtual viewings | Convert | Local; diaspora (virtual) | Attendees to second viewing | Schedule diaspora sessions for UK and North America evenings |
| LinkedIn | Create | Corporates, investors | Qualified leads | Lease and investment angles |
| PR, property media, roadshows | Create | Flagship launches and developments only | Coverage, event leads | Not worth it for single resale listings |

**Budget split default:** 70% proven channels (from CRM history), 20% tests, 10% experiments. Without history, weight toward portals and Meta, and declare it an assumption.

## 5. Pre-launch asset checklist

- Professional photos (daylight plus twilight), floor plan, 3D or virtual tour
- Video: 60 to 90 second cut and a 5 to 8 minute full walk-through
- Drone footage only after confirming current permit requirements (NCAA and security clearance)
- Spec sheet: power (grid band, solar, inverter, generator), water, security, service charge
- Location sheet: peak-hour drive times to VI, Ikoyi, Lekki toll axis, airport, key schools
- Trust pack: title type, survey, search report on request, practitioner registration
- Canonical listing copy and price, shared with every co-broke agent
- ARCON approval for paid creative; consent-checked contact lists

## 6. Funnel and KPIs

Back-solve from the close target using CRM conversion rates for the segment and price band.

```
offers needed     = closes needed / (offer -> close rate)
viewings needed   = offers needed / (viewing -> offer rate)
qualified needed  = viewings needed / (qualified -> viewing rate)
inquiries needed  = qualified needed / (inquiry -> qualified rate)
```

Round each stage up. If CRM rates are missing, use these placeholders and tag them `[Assumption]`: inquiry to qualified 25%, qualified to viewing 35%, viewing to offer 15%, offer to close 50%.

**Qualified lead** = budget confirmed within the band, purchase timeline 6 months or less, decision-maker (or a mandated proxy) identified, funding route known.

**Weekly dashboard:** spend, inquiries, qualified leads, cost per qualified lead, viewings, offers, median response time, top 3 objections from viewing feedback.

## 7. Budget

```
variable (lead generation) = qualified leads needed x target cost per qualified lead
fixed                      = production + events + portal subscriptions not tied to leads
contingency                = about 10% of variable + fixed
total                      = variable + fixed + contingency
```

- Target cost per qualified lead comes from CRM history; otherwise `[Assumption]`.
- Leadership version only: total as % of expected fee (expected close price x commission rate). Default guardrail: flag when above 25% and propose a leaner mix.
- Client version: show the total and anything the owner pays. Never show the fee ratio.

## 8. Timeline and review gates

Scale to the close target. Defaults for a 90-day sale:

| When | Gate | Threshold | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Week 0 | Launch readiness | Assets, trust pack, canonical listing, ARCON approval, agent briefing done | If ARCON approval is pending, launch portals, agents, and organic first; start paid when approved |
| Day 14 | Traffic | Qualified leads below 50% of pro-rata target | Fix creative, targeting, or channel mix. If leads are on target but viewings lag, fix response time and qualification |
| Day 30 | Price | Viewings on target but no offer, or price cited as the main objection in 40% or more of viewing feedback | Take the pre-approved price step |
| Day 60 | Strategy | Below 50% of cumulative funnel target after the price step | Fallback: lease, reposition to another segment, or withdraw, refresh, and relaunch |

Get the price step and the fallback approved by the owner before launch. That turns a later price conversation into executing an agreed rule.

**Seasonality:** many diaspora Nigerians visit Lagos in December. If the campaign spans December, schedule in-person diaspora viewings then and start diaspora advertising 4 to 6 weeks earlier.

## 9. Portfolio mode (several listings, one budget)

1. For each listing: priority = expected fee x probability of closing in the window / marketing cost.
2. Fund in priority order until the budget runs out.
3. Group listings that share a segment into shared campaigns (one diaspora campaign, several listings).
4. Report unfunded listings and what it would take to fund them.

## 10. Compliance flags (include in every plan)

- **ARCON:** paid adverts, including social media and influencer posts, need pre-exposure vetting. Build the lead time into Week 0.
- **NDPA 2023:** lawful basis for collecting and using lead data, opt-out on every broadcast, data retention limits, consent records for WhatsApp and email lists.
- **LASRERA:** practitioners and agents on the transaction should be registered.
- **Advertising accuracy:** describe the title exactly (for example, never write "C of O" when the title is Governor's Consent or pending). Misleading property claims expose the company to consumer-protection action.
- Close with: "Confirm current requirements with compliance before launch."
