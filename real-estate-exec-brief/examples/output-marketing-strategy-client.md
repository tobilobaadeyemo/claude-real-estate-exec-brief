## Presenter Notes (client meeting)

**Opening:** "You asked us to get the best result for this home. Our own sales say the best result comes from pricing at the market, not above it. Homes we launched above market sold 12% under their list price and took almost three months. Homes launched at market sold 3% under list in under two months. So ₦595M is the price most likely to put the most money in your hand, by January."

**Three numbers:** ₦586.5M market. ₦595M list. ₦560M your floor.
**Concede:** we cannot guarantee a sale date; we commit to the reviews and the actions each triggers.
**Do not concede:** listing at ₦650M "to test the market". Show A4.

**Likely questions**
- *"My house is worth more."* Walk through C1 to C3: closed sales, adjusted for your renovation and plot. Then offer the agreed price step as the safeguard.
- *"Why drop to ₦595M and not ₦620M?"* ₦620M is above the ₦600M search band, so fewer buyers see it, and it sits 5.7% above market: the zone where our history shows slower, lower sales.
- *"Who sees my documents?"* Only verified buyers, through our solicitor. Listings state the title type only.
- *"What if nobody buys by January?"* The 11 Dec review decides between leasing at about ₦30M a year and a refreshed relaunch, with your approval.
