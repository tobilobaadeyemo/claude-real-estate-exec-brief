## Evidence
Supporting signals:
1. Gross yield of 8.1% is 1.6 points above the segment median (rule: lease when at least 1 point above and rents stable or rising).
2. Real rents up 5.2% year on year (nominal +21%, CPI 15.0%) [Internal leases; portal asking rents; NBS].
3. Sale days on market up 20% year on year: a sale now means a longer campaign into a softening sale market.
4. Hold-and-lease nominal return of 16.0% (6.0% net yield plus 10.0% price trend) clears the 15% hurdle [Internal].

Strongest counter-signal: real prices are down 4.3%, and the margin over the hurdle is thin (1 point). If nominal appreciation drops below 9%, hold-and-lease falls below the hurdle. Hence the 31 Mar 2027 re-test.
