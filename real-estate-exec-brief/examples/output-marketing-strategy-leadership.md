## Presenter Notes

**Opening (60 seconds):** "We're recommending ₦595M, not ₦650M, and our own deals show why. When we launch within 5% of market, we close about 3% below list in roughly 52 days. When we launch above market, we close about 12% below list in roughly 88 days. At ₦650M, history says the owner ends near ₦572M, two months later. At ₦595M, near ₦577M, by January. We'll spend ₦5.4M, under a fifth of the expected fee, to produce 40 qualified leads, 14 viewings, and 2 offers. If it isn't working by 11 November, we step to ₦575M, a rule the owner agrees to upfront. I need approval on price, budget, and the step rule today."

**Three numbers:** ₦586.5M market median. ₦595M list. ₦5.4M budget.
**Concede if pushed:** confidence is medium; only 3 comps are closed sales.
**Do not concede:** listing above the ₦600M band.

**Likely questions**
- *Why not list at ₦650M and negotiate?* Our own above-market launches closed 12% below list after 88 days. A4 has sat for 170 days at an above-market ask.
- *Is ₦5.4M worth it?* 18.7% of expected fee, with a ₦65k cost per qualified lead target against a ₦72k 12-month average.
- *Why diaspora?* 30% of qualified inquiries, up from 21%, and December brings them to Lagos.
- *Supply is up 21%. Should we wait?* Real prices are flat, so waiting adds holding cost without adding value. Pricing at market is the defense against more supply.
- *What if there are no offers by 11 November?* Step to ₦575M, pre-approved. The 11 December gate decides lease or relaunch.
